Every coding-agent session starts from zero: the agent forgets yesterday's decisions, the reasons behind them, and the next step. After two weeks on a real project, I settled on three layers: a markdown memory bank for where we are right now, jevmem for facts that stay true, and `AGENTS.md` for the rules the agent must always follow. Keeping them separate is what stops state from going stale in long-term memory and tokens from being spent twice.
